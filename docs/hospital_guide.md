# Facility & Room Navigation Guide

## Overview
This document serves as the primary knowledge base for the HumanCallRobot's RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) AI assistant system.

## Room Locations & Directories
- **Room 101**: Patient Room located on Floor 1 along the East Wing Corridor.
- **Room 102**: Patient Room located on Floor 1, 3 meters east of Room 101.
- **Room 103**: Patient Room located on Floor 1, 6 meters east of Room 101.
- **Nurse Station**: Located at the central intersection near the main lobby entrance.
- **Lobby**: Main visitor entrance on Floor 1.
- **Charging Station**: Located at coordinates (-1.0, -1.0) near the maintenance room.

## Emergency Protocols & Visiting Hours
- **Visiting Hours**: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily.
- **Emergency Button**: Pressing any wall-mounted call button immediately dispatches the HumanCallRobot to your exact room location.
